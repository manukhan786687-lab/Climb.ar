HAMMERCLIMB

One pot. One hammer. One long way up.

BEST HEIGHT 0m

HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag your finger (or mouse) around your character to aim the hammer.
  • Press and hold to actively swing the hammer toward your finger.
  • The hammer pushes against platforms and walls — use it to pull and lever your pot upward.
  • There is no walking. Momentum and gravity are your only friends.
  • Release to let the hammer swing freely, like a pendulum.
  • Falling can send you back down — but checkpoints will save your progress (unless Hard Mode is on).
  • Reach the tower at the very top to win.
